Questions

How do I play?
Hold the left side of the screen to move. On the right: Attack, Roll and Mending Vial. Hold the phone in landscape.
Where is my progress saved?
When you rest at a campfire. Tap Continue on the title screen to return to your last campfire. Progress stays on this iPhone only.
I fell. What happens to my Embers?
They wait where you fell. Walk back over them to recover them. If you fall again first, they are gone.
Why did the monsters come back?
Resting at a campfire heals you and refills your vials, and the monsters return. Bosses you have defeated stay defeated.
Which devices work?
iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
