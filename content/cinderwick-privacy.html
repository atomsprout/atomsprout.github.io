Cinderwick (called Foxwick before version 1.7) does not collect your name, email address, location or contacts, and it has no account of its own. It is a single-player iPhone game published by AtomSprout. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves it is a record of a Full Journey purchase, described below.

Information we collect

When you buy or restore the Full Journey in-app purchase, a record of that purchase goes to RevenueCat, the service AtomSprout uses to see its sales. The record includes the product, price, currency, date, App Store storefront and transaction identifiers from Apple, plus a random identifier that RevenueCat creates for this install. It also includes basic technical details: app version, iOS version, device model and language. It is not linked to your name, email address or Apple Account, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. The game does not collect how you play, and it asks for no permissions.

Data stored on your device

Your class, cleared stages, defeated bosses, Embers, skills, charms, medals, last campfire and settings (music, sound effects, haptics, damage numbers) are saved on your iPhone so Continue can bring you back. AtomSprout never receives this data.

In-app purchase

Stages 1 to 10 are free. The Full Journey is a one-time in-app purchase that opens stages 11 to 20. Apple processes the payment; AtomSprout never sees your payment details. Whether the Full Journey is open is decided by your Apple Account’s purchases, so Restore Purchases works on any device signed in to it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple’s Game Center. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy, and your Game Center profile may show it to friends or other players according to your Game Center settings. You can turn Game Center off in the Settings app.

Third parties

RevenueCat, Inc. processes the purchase records above on AtomSprout’s behalf, under its privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple processes payments, App Store data and Game Center data under its own terms and privacy policies. The game contains no advertising, no analytics of how you play and no tracking, and it does not sell or share data with data brokers. The links in Settings open a web page in your browser only when you tap them.

Retention and deletion

Saved progress stays on your iPhone until you start a new game or delete the app. Deleting the game removes everything it stored on the device. Purchase records are kept while needed for sales reporting and accounting. To ask for the purchase records tied to your install to be deleted, email us with the date of the purchase.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data it sends is the purchase record described above, which does not identify the player.

Changes and contact

If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant release. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email atomsprout@gmail.com. This policy is effective from 8 November 2026.
