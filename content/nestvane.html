Nestvane

Android, running inside an app on your iPhone.

Nestvane starts a full Android 17 system in an iPhone app and shows it on your screen. Install Android apps from APK files, use them with touch, and keep their data between launches.

What works · Limits · Community · Privacy policy

What works today

  • Android 17. The home screen, Settings and Files, built from the Android Open Source Project.
  • APK files. Open an APK with Nestvane from another app, or choose Install APK in Nestvane.
  • Network. Android apps use your iPhone’s Wi-Fi or cellular connection.
  • Shared files. Android’s shared storage is a folder in the Files app: On My iPhone → Nestvane → Android. Files you put there appear in Android the next time it starts.
  • Home screen widgets. Add widgets from installed apps to the Android home screen.
  • Video and sound. Android apps play video, and their sound comes out of your iPhone.
  • Your iPhone’s clock and battery. Android shows your time zone and battery level.

Limits

  • Nestvane is in private testing and is not available to download yet.
  • Video is decoded in software. 720p video plays smoothly with sound; higher resolutions have not been measured.
  • There is no recording, and Android apps cannot use the microphone.
  • There are no Google apps or Google Play services. Apps that require them will not run.
  • Apps that bring their own native code run noticeably slower than on an Android phone.
  • Some apps do not start. Tell us which ones matter to you.

Requirements

iOS 26 or later. Nestvane has been tested on iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro.

Community and support

Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026.

What Nestvane collects

Nothing. Nestvane has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send your files, your installed apps or your activity to AtomSprout.

Network

When Nestvane starts, it makes one request to Apple’s connectivity check at captive.apple.com so that iOS can ask whether Nestvane may use the network. Android itself checks its connection the way Android devices do, by contacting Google’s connectivity check servers. Everything else on the network is traffic of the Android apps you install, which handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Your data

Android’s apps and their data are stored inside Nestvane on your iPhone. Shared files are in the Nestvane folder of the Files app. Deleting Nestvane removes all of it.
