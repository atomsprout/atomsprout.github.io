Privacy

Your typing is yours.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What Remoboard does

Remoboard turns its iOS keyboard extension or Android keyboard service into a small local web server. A browser on the same local network can pair with the keyboard and send text or explicit clipboard commands directly to the phone.

Developer-operated services

Remoboard does not require an account and does not route typed text, pairing PINs, clipboard content, quick words, or connection diagnostics through a server operated by the developer. The app does not use advertising trackers.

Data stored on your device

Settings, quick words, snippets, trusted-computer records and the keyboard theme are stored locally in the Remoboard app group. For trusted computers the phone keeps only a one-way hash of each access token, the browser label you see in the list (for example “Chrome · macOS”) and when it was last used. Send history and the trusted-computer token are kept in your computer’s browser storage for that page only. Connection diagnostics, when available, are stored locally with text payloads redacted. You control whether to export diagnostic information.

In-app purchases

Remoboard Pro is an optional in-app purchase. On iOS, purchases are processed by Apple. The Remoboard host app (never the keyboard extension) uses RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them across your devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your App Store transaction and receipt information, the app version and basic device information (such as OS version and locale). It never receives your typed text, clipboard content, quick words, snippets or diagnostics. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy. The host app checks your purchase status with RevenueCat when it opens; the keyboard only reads the cached result on your device.

Local network and Full Access

iOS requires “Allow Full Access” for a third-party keyboard to communicate over the local network. Remoboard uses that access to accept connections from your browser. PIN pairing is on by default: a browser must enter the code shown on the keyboard before it can type. You can turn off this requirement in the app settings. If you do, other devices that can reach the local address may connect, so use that option only on networks you trust.

Remoboard for Android

The Android app works the same way and follows the same rules: no account, no advertising trackers, and typed text, PINs, clipboard content, quick words, snippets and diagnostics stay on your phone and your local network. Android shows a standard warning when any third-party keyboard is enabled; Remoboard only types what a paired browser sends and does not record your keystrokes. On Android 17 and later the app asks for the “nearby devices / local network” permission, which it uses solely to accept connections from your browser. Settings are stored in the app’s private storage and may be included in your device’s Android backup. On Android, the Pro features are currently included for free: the app does not offer a purchase and does not contact RevenueCat or any other purchase service. If Remoboard Pro is sold on Android in the future, purchases will be processed by Google Play and validated through RevenueCat in the same way as on iOS, and this policy will be updated before that change ships.

Clipboard access

Clipboard content (text or, with Remoboard Pro, images) is read or written only when you explicitly use a clipboard action in the paired browser interface, or insert a snippet that contains the {clipboard} variable. Remote clipboard reads and writes can each be turned off in the app. Clipboard content travels over the direct local connection.

Third-party destinations

If you follow links from this site to the App Store, GitHub, or Discord, those services apply their own privacy policies. The Remoboard app itself does not send your typed content to them.

Email feedback

If you choose Email Feedback, Remoboard opens a draft addressed to atomsprout@gmail.com in your mail app. The draft includes the app name, version and build, operating system version, non-unique device model and preferred language. You can review or edit it and add your own message before sending. Remoboard does not send the draft automatically or attach typed text, clipboard content, logs, documents or account details. Your chosen email provider processes the message under its own privacy policy, and the developer receives the message you send. If no mail app is available, you can copy the draft instead.
