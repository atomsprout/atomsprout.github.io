D-Weave

A voice keyboard for Mandarin and English on iPhone and iPad.

Speak in either language or mix them in one sentence. D-Weave turns your speech into text on your device and inserts it where you are typing.

Support · Privacy policy

Support

  1. Open D-Weave and allow microphone access when prompted.
  2. In Settings, go to General → Keyboard → Keyboards. Add D-Weave, then turn on Allow Full Access for the D-Weave keyboard.
  3. Open a text field and hold the globe key to switch to D-Weave. Tap Dictate to start and Finish to insert the transcript.

D-Weave requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. The microphone and Allow Full Access settings are both required for keyboard dictation.

Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026.

Microphone and transcription

D-Weave uses the microphone when you start dictation. Audio is processed in memory by the speech model bundled with the keyboard. D-Weave does not send audio or transcripts to AtomSprout or a third-party transcription service. D-Weave does not save recordings or maintain a dictation history.

Keyboard permissions

D-Weave needs the iOS Allow Full Access setting to use the microphone in the keyboard. Speech recognition runs on the device. You can revoke microphone permission or turn off Allow Full Access in Settings.

Text you insert

When you finish dictating, the keyboard inserts the transcript into your current text field. The app receiving the text handles it under its own privacy policy. D-Weave has no account, advertising, or in-app tracking.
