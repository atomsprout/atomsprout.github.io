What we collect

Nothing. Foxwick has no account, no sign-in and no server. It does not collect your name, email, location or how you play.

How your data is used

Your class, cleared stages, defeated bosses, Embers and last campfire are saved in storage on your iPhone, so Continue can bring you back. That data never leaves your device.

Third parties

Foxwick includes no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests, and your data is shared with no one.

Retention and deletion

Saved progress stays on your iPhone until you start a new game or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of it. There is nothing for us to delete, because we never receive it.

Withdrawing consent

Delete Foxwick to remove everything it stored. The game asks for no permissions, so there is nothing else to switch off.
