GrapeCompare support

Installation and requirements

GrapeCompare requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Install it from the Mac App Store, the only supported distribution channel. Homebrew and older downloaded builds are not supported.

How do I start a comparison?

Drop two files or two folders on the window, or choose them with the Choose buttons. You can also use Finder Open With, the file shelf, or the Compare Files Shortcut. For a three-way merge, choose the base, left and right inputs yourself. Git integration, difftool/mergetool hooks and a command-line tool are not included.

GrapeCompare can't open a file

The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and can only open items you select. Choose the file or folder again with the Choose button or drag it onto the window, then repeat the operation.

GrapeCompare Pro and purchases

Every Pro feature can be tried three times for free. Buy Pro or choose Restore Purchases from GrapeCompare Pro in the app menu or in Settings. If you downloaded GrapeCompare before version 1.1.0, Pro is already unlocked for you. Manage or cancel the yearly plan in your Apple Account subscriptions; Apple handles refunds.

AI change summary is missing

The summary uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac and appears only on macOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on. Diff content is never sent to the developer or a cloud service.

Report a problem

Update to the latest Mac App Store version first. Then email atomsprout@gmail.com with the GrapeCompare version, macOS version, Mac model, steps to reproduce, and approximate file sizes or folder item counts. Please don't attach confidential comparison content. You can also join our Discord.
