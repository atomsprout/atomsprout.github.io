Questions

Cinderwick was called Foxwick until version 1.7. It is the same game, and your progress and purchases carry over. When you write, include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

How do I play?
Hold the phone in landscape. Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to move, and push the stick up (or up and to either side) to jump. On the right: Attack, Roll, Mending Vial, three skill buttons and the gold Ultimate button, which fills as you fight and glows when it is ready. Hold Attack to charge a heavy blow as the Samurai or Mage. The Assassin deals double damage when striking from behind. Near a campfire with no enemies close by, Attack becomes Rest.
Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone, automatically. Continue on the title screen returns you to your last campfire. New Game clears your campfires and defeated bosses. Progress does not sync between devices, and deleting the app removes it.
I fell. What happens to my Embers?
They wait where you fell. Walk back over them to recover them. If you fall again first, they are gone.
Why did the monsters come back?
Resting at a campfire saves your progress and refills your vials, and the monsters return. Bosses you have defeated stay defeated.
How do skills, charms and the Camp work?
Skill tomes lie along the road; picking one up teaches a new skill. Tap Camp on the Stage Map, or rest at a campfire, to choose your three skills, raise their ranks, wear charms and trade Embers at the fox’s wares. Every boss you beat also gives a free skill rank. The Journal on the title screen keeps the bestiary, achievements and records.
When do the other modes open?
The Ember Trial and the Daily Trial open after the stage 3 boss, and the Boss Rush after the Bell Tower boss. Beat the Sunheart at stage 20 (part of the Full Journey) to begin New Journey+, which keeps your hero and makes every foe tougher.

The Full Journey

Stages 1 to 10, through the Bell Tower Summit, are free. The Full Journey is a one-time in-app purchase that opens stages 11 to 20, the twenty-boss Rush, New Journey+ and the deepest Ember Trial bosses. Open it from the gold Full Journey button on the second page of the Stage Map, or from stage 11 once the Bell Tower boss is down.

Bought it on another iPhone, or reinstalled the game? Tap the gear on the title screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple handles payments and refunds; to ask for a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Cinderwick reports 26 achievements and three leaderboards (Ember Trial depth and both Boss Rush times). Game Center is optional; turning it off in the Settings app changes nothing else in the game.

Settings

Tap the gear on the title screen to turn music, sound effects, haptics and damage numbers on or off. The game also follows Reduce Motion in iOS Settings, which removes screen shake and knockback.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Cinderwick plays in landscape and works fully offline; buying or restoring the Full Journey needs an internet connection.
