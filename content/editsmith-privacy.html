Privacy by design

Privacy Policy

EditSmith keeps actions and settings on your Mac and makes every model choice explicit.

01
No developer-operated service

EditSmith does not send source text, actions, test fixtures, settings, or usage data to a server operated by the developer.

02
Local execution

JavaScript actions run locally through JavaScriptCore. Apple On-Device actions use Foundation Models. XcodeKit supplies text only when you invoke an action.

03
Optional model providers

A user-invoked PCC or Ollama action may send selected text—or the full buffer with no selection—to that chosen provider.

04
Local storage

Actions, prompts, provider configuration, fixtures, settings, and the latest undo snapshot stay in the local shared container.

05
Outgoing connections only

The app and extension connect outward only for explicitly selected PCC or Ollama actions. They accept no inbound connections.

Questions may be sent to atomsprout@gmail.com.
